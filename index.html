About Us

Building Future IT Professionals Since 2021

Founded by Mr. Aruri Sravan, SVR Computer Education has grown from a small training center to a leading IT education institute in Hanamkonda, Telangana. With over 15 years of experience in the IT industry and education sector, we bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements.
