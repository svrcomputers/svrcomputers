Our Journey

Founded in 2021 by Mr. Aruri Sravan, SVR Computer Education has grown from a small training center to a leading IT education institute in Hanamkonda.

With a vision to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements, we focus on practical, hands-on training that prepares students for real-world challenges.

Over 500+ students have successfully completed our courses and are now working in top IT companies across India.
